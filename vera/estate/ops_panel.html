Pipes

Runs follow each plane's own lanes. Between planes a run leaves by its plane's bus point on the back edge, on a lane of its own, rises or falls on a riser outside every plate, and wears its origin plane's colour. Selecting a node lights the shortest route from it to every end it reaches, down to the metal and up to the work, and fades the rest; Esc, a click on empty space or Clear on the plot puts it back.

Widgets▾

A node carries several widgets: a server for a machine, and its readings — a dial for its load, a thermometer, a tank for its disk — each joined to the node on the plane. Select a node and press ⊕ to add one; the size button steps ×¾ · ×1 · ×1.3; ✕ takes one off.
